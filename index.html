Overview

Set above Lake Washington in Kirkland, this custom residence pairs sweeping lake and mountain views with thoughtful, high-end craftsmanship throughout its 6,310 square feet. Five bedrooms and five bathrooms are arranged across a main-floor primary suite, private guest wings, and generous lower-level living — all finished with the same quiet attention to detail, inside and out.

Main Living Area

Great Room

The great room rises to 12-foot ceilings beneath tray and low-voltage lighting, its solid doors and custom shades framing a linear fireplace set into a wall of smoky slate. Surround sound carries throughout, and a 10-by-5 sliding door wall opens to the patio and view beyond.

Joseph Sidof established Shakúff in 2007 after working in the costume jewelry industry and developing a love for glassblowing. Working closely with residential and commercial clients, Shakúff lighting aims to explore and enhance the natural beauty of light, creating custom light systems through impeccable craftsmanship and innovative artistry.
Kitchen

Chef's Kitchen

Italian Carrera gold countertops meet Sun Valley bronze hardware and an oversized island, anchored by a Sub-Zero refrigerator and freezer, two Miele dishwashers, and an eight-burner Wolf range with double ovens. On-demand hot water runs throughout, alongside a built-in buffet, a butler's wine pantry, and a walk-in pantry fitted for 142 bottles.

Primary Suite

A Private Wing

Heated floors carry through a primary suite centered on a spacious dual-head shower, TOTO toilet and bidet, and double vanities set beneath custom lighting and Grohe fixtures. A soaking spa tub anchors the room, and a custom-fitted closet with built-in shelving and dressers completes the space. Sliding doors open directly onto the patio and the view beyond.

Every finish in the suite was chosen to frame the water and mountains beyond — quiet, private, and entirely its own wing of the home.

Home & Gardens

Grounds & Gardens

The grounds are finished in real stucco beneath a Presidential 50-year roof, with gorgeous landscaping surrounding vegetable and rose gardens and a private greenhouse.

Two decks extend the living space outdoors, warmed by heat lamps and a fireplace and stocked with an ice maker for effortless entertaining, all beneath a covered porch with sweeping views over the water.
